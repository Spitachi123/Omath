Pratap Malla contributed a lot to Nepali culture during his reign. Can you name some of his contributions that still exist today? Write your own answer first.

Hanuman Dhoka PalaceNamed after the Hanuman statue he placed at the gate; still the historic royal palace of Kathmandu Durbar Square.
Rani PokhariThe pond he built in Kathmandu to console his grieving queen after the loss of their son.
Krishna Temple & Taleju CourtyardTemples he renovated and embellished within Durbar Square.
The Fifteen-Language Stone InscriptionA stone slab near Hanuman Dhoka engraved with verses in fifteen languages, said to gush milk if read fluently.
Nepal Bhasa & Sanskrit PoetryHis literary works and dramas, composed under his pen name "Kabindra," remain part of Nepal's literary heritage.
