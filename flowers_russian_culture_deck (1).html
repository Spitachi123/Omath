Chapter One

Red carnations: flowers that fought

Patriotism, unbowed Red = defiant courage Protests · memorials
  • Patriotic symbol — rooted in the 1917 February Revolution.
  • Bolshevik badge — worn as carnation-shaped cardboard pins.
  • Symbol of resistance — defiance against the Provisional Government.
  • Veterans & memorials — gifted to honor soldiers, living and fallen.
  • Bold red — reads as courage and pride, never romance.

Мак · Mak — Poppy
Chapter Two

White carnations & red poppies: beauty with a warning label

Bad omens · fleeting love White = ill-omen · Red = passion Performances · weddings
  • Stage bad omen — white carnations are said to strip luck from performers.
  • Dream inspiration — tucked under a pillow to invite vivid dreams.
  • Fleeting love — red poppies signal a weak, short-lived feeling.
  • Avoid gifting — poppies carry too sorrowful a note for romance.
  • Wedding prosperity — poppy seeds hidden in shoes or food bless a marriage.

Chapter Three

Yellow flowers & tulips: the bouquet of broken promises

Betrayal & separation Yellow = dishonesty Romance — avoid
  • Cultural taboo — the opposite of "friendship yellow" in the West.
  • Sign of separation — implies sadness, infidelity, dishonesty.
  • Yellow tulips specifically — read as a literal breakup warning.
  • Pop culture impact — a famous song cemented the taboo nationwide.
  • Gift etiquette — never hand these to a partner or spouse.

Ромашка · Romashka
Chapter Four

Chamomile: Russia's bloom for body and soul

National flower · healing White petals, gold center Medicine · protection
  • National flower — Russia's officially recognized bloom.
  • Distinct look — slender white petals, bright yellow center.
  • Medicinal tea — steeped for centuries as a gentle remedy.
  • Aromatherapy use — oils used well beyond a simple cup of tea.
  • Pest control — burned as a natural insect-repelling smoke.

Chapter Five

Petals of fortune-telling: he loves me...

Divination & hope Dainty white petals Personal rituals
  • Romantic divination — "he loves me, he loves me not," petal by petal.
  • Inner reflection — a quiet, self-murmured ritual for young women.
  • Lily rituals — lily of the valley used the same way, for broader fortune.
  • Outcome pattern — "it will happen, it won't happen."
  • Dainty symbolism — delicate white petals mean open-ended hope.
Калла · Kalla
Chapter Six

White calla lily: wedding-white, funeral-bound

Mourning · bad omen Pure white Weddings — forbidden
  • Visual paradox — matches wedding-dress white, but not the meaning.
  • Association with death — tightly linked to funerals and mourning.
  • Wedding taboo — bringing these to a wedding is a real misstep.
  • Foreshadowing separation — believed to curse newlyweds' future.
  • Strict avoidance — kept away from every celebratory gathering.
